# Locally Coherent, Globally Incoherent: Bounding Compositional Incoherence in Multi-Component LLM Agents

- **Authors:** Anany Kotawala
- **Published:** 2026-05-28
- **Categories:** cs.AI, cs.CL
- **arXiv:** [http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30335v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30335v1)
- **PDF:** [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.30335v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.30335v1)

## Abstract

Multi-component LLM agents assemble probabilistic claims from components that each see only part of a joint problem; the composition can violate basic probability axioms even when every component is locally coherent. We formalise this locally coherent, globally incoherent failure via the compositional residual eps*, the L2 distance from the composed quote to the joint coherent polytope, computable at runtime from system output and the declared cross-component coupling constraints. A product-structure dichotomy characterises when local coherence suffices, and a Rayleigh-quotient prediction matches the observed residual within 7% on three of four relation classes. A hierarchical Boyle-Dykstra projection repairs the composition deterministically; an anytime-valid e-process gives sequential coherence monitoring. Across 1,876 ensemble cliques on a four-LLM mid-tier panel (frontier-panel rerun in Section 5.5), eps* > 0 on 33-94% of cliques, translating to +0.115 nats per bet of regret on 1,770 resolved bets under the proportional allocation rule (the gain collapses to +0.006 under bettors that themselves coherentise). Three intuitive LLM-side mitigations(retrieval, partition-aware prompting, aggregator-LLM) each fail or regress.

## Research content

The paper formalizes a failure mode in multi-component LLM agents where individual components produce locally coherent probabilistic claims, but their composition violates basic probability axioms globally. The authors introduce the compositional residual eps*, an L2 distance measure from composed output to the joint coherent polytope, which can be computed at runtime. They establish a product-structure dichotomy characterizing when local coherence suffices, and demonstrate that a Rayleigh-quotient prediction matches observed residual within 7% on three of four relation classes. They propose a hierarchical Boyle-Dykstra projection to deterministically repair compositions and an anytime-valid e-process for sequential coherence monitoring. Empirical evaluation across 1,876 ensemble cliques on a four-LLM panel shows eps* > 0 on 33-94% of cliques, with significant betting regret implications.

## Problem addressed

Multi-component LLM agents assemble probabilistic claims from components that each see only part of a joint problem, leading to compositions that violate basic probability axioms even when every component is locally coherent.

## Limitations

- Three intuitive LLM-side mitigations (retrieval, partition-aware prompting, aggregator-LLM) each fail or regress
- The Rayleigh-quotient prediction does not match observed residual within 7% on one of four relation classes
- The gain from compositional incoherence collapses to +0.006 nats per bet when bettors themselves coherentise

## Possible improvement directions

- The hierarchical Boyle-Dykstra projection for deterministic repair of compositions could be further developed
- The anytime-valid e-process for sequential coherence monitoring could be extended
- Alternative mitigations beyond the three tested LLM-side approaches may be needed
